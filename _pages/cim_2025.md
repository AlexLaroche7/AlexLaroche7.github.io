I ran 2:25:33 at the California International Marathon on December 7, 2025, with a 74-minute first half and a 71-minute second half. It was my second marathon, and I improved my personal best by 16 minutes (2:41 to 2:25). Here is a summary of the 12-week training block leading up to the race. My coach at the time, Stephane Colle, organized the build into three 4-week blocks. I averaged 107 km per week.

The main lesson for me was that 2:25 took less than I expected. I had run more volume in the past and raced worse. What made the difference this time was staying fresh and dosing the quality carefully: moderate (some would say low) mileage with two or three quality sessions per week. I have dealt with setbacks since I started running, partly because I began trying to run competitively while 70 lbs heavier, and partly from later bouts of overreaching. Getting through a full block without one mattered a lot. The block also built on a summer of mostly base training, with little work above threshold, while I trained for a 50k trail race. My best times the year before (2024) were 16:10 for 5k and 33:30 for 10k.
